%--- PROFILE ---
\section*{Profile}

Researcher in Trustworthy and Explainable AI (XAI), specializing in explanation generation, evaluation, and correction for black-box models, out-of-distribution (OOD) detection, and trustworthy deployment in high-stakes domains (healthcare, industrial manufacturing). PhD in Information and Communication Technologies (University of Granada, 2025); dissertation delivers three pillars aligned with the Trustworthy AI agenda: REVEL (explanation evaluation framework), X-SHIELD (explanation-driven regularization), and STOOD-X (explainable OOD detection with statistical confidence). Early-impact trajectory: 521 citations, COVID-19 chest X-ray dataset (477 citations), sustained XAI publications in top venues, open-source releases, and active engagement with ADIA Lab as a speaker at the ADIA Lab International Summer School on Explainable AI (Granada, 2025).
